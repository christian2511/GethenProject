Interstellar Travel Guide to Gethen (Winter)

Gethen—designated Winter in Hainish Ekumen reports—is a harsh, ice-bound world inhabited by ambisexual humans. This guide covers its two dominant nations: Karhide and Orgoreyn.

KARHIDE

A fragmented, feudal realm of deep-rooted clan loyalties, ancient customs, and dramatic mountain scenery. One of the oldest stable civilizations on the planet.

History
Populations grew organically out of family clans and hearth-societies along mountain passes. This fostered a decentralized political structure directed by a King and a Prime Minister, with ultimate authority balanced by the regional Domains.
Why Important
Karhide is the emotional and cultural seat of Gethenian traditionalism. Crucially, it is the nation where the Ekumen made historic first contact, testing whether an ambisexual human society could integrate into galactic civilization.
Significant Construction
To survive ferocious sub-zero winters, architecture relies heavily on immense stone fortifications, thick walls, and communal hearth-living. Key landmarks include the ancient royal city of Sinth Herval and isolated cliff-side sanctuaries like Sator Hawi.
Expression
Karhide speech is poetic and elaborate, layered with double meanings, political maneuvering, and legalistic nuances. Art and dance are deeply intertwined with seasonal festivals and clan pride.
Sights
Towering mountain ranges, winding glacier valleys, and historic hearth-towns nestled deep in alpine passes.
Activities
Participate in seasonal clan meetings, master the political art of local debate, or embark on guided ice treks across high mountain passes.
To Eat
Hearty winter fare consisting of smoked river fish, pickled root vegetables, dense barley breads, and hot, spiced brewed beverages to beat the bitter cold.
Accommodations
Traditional clan hearth-inns where travelers share a communal blaze, swap stories, and find shelter from howling storms.

ORGOREYN

A vast, highly organized, and industrialized state on a sweeping plateau. In sharp contrast to Karhide's feudal clans, Orgoreyn is governed by a council of thirty-three Commensals who oversee a unified collective bureaucracy.

History
Evolving from a federation of agricultural and industrial communes, Orgoreyn modernized rapidly through centralized planning, massive public works, and collective labor.
Why Important
Orgoreyn represents the pinnacle of Gethenian mass organization, social engineering, and economic production. Its tense ideological rivalry with Karhide shapes modern Gethenian geopolitics.
Significant Construction
Dominated by utilitarian architecture, sprawling collective farms, vast factory complexes, and massive subterranean or heavily insulated housing blocks designed to efficiently shelter large workforces.
Expression
Official state communication values clarity, collective productivity, and ideological harmony, though it is underlaid by a labyrinth of secret police, rival factions, and silent purges (notably the Farm work camps).
Sights
Sweeping mechanized agricultural plains, monolithic municipal government seats, and glowing industrial hubs standing out against the frozen wasteland.
Activities
Tour state-run industrial cooperatives, study advanced municipal planning, or observe government-sponsored cultural festivals.
To Eat
Standardized, highly nutritious communal meals featuring processed grains, protein pastes, root vegetable stews, and fermented drinks served in vast communal dining halls.
Accommodations
Functional, state-run municipal dormitories and traveler hostels offering clean, uniform rooms with standard municipal services.

Surviving the Ice

GEAR

  • Sledge & harness for transport
  • Double-walled tent
  • Layered furs
  • Camp stove (for melting water)
  • Snowshoes
  • Rope
  • Ice axes

MOVEMENT & SAFETY

  • Pace yourself to avoid sweating (sweat causes hypothermia)
  • Stay roped together over crevasses
  • Stop immediately during whiteouts

HEALTH & NUTRITION

  • Consume high-calorie rations
  • Wear slitted goggles to prevent snow blindness
  • Stick to a strict daily routine to combat sensory deprivation
